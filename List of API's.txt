Society Maintenance & Payment Management System
Developed a Spring Boot-based backend application to automate monthly maintenance billing, payment tracking, late-penalty calculation, outstanding dues, and society-level collection reporting for a 35-flat residential society.

Then your technical bullets can become:

Designed REST APIs using Java, Spring Boot, Spring Data JPA and PostgreSQL for society, wing, flat, member, maintenance and payment management.
Automated monthly maintenance calculation based on flat area × configurable per-square-foot maintenance rate.
Implemented monthly billing, due-date tracking, late-payment penalties and flat-wise outstanding balance calculation.
Developed role-based access for society administrators and members, enabling members to view bills/payment history and administrators to monitor collections.
Built reporting workflows for monthly collection, pending dues, payment history and audit reconciliation.
Designed relational entity mappings between Society → Wing → Flat → Member → Maintenance → Payment.
